01 / 04Applied analytics · Community logistics

Zach Johnson Foundation · COVID-19 response

Balancing meal deliveries across ~18 volunteer drivers

About 200 delivery locations needed practical routes. I used k-nearest-neighbor logic as a starting point, then balanced time, vehicle capacity, safer delivery conditions, and volunteer feedback.

Read the routing case study ↗

What I coordinated

Community partnerships, shipments, staggered hub loading, food distribution, and routing for roughly 18 drivers. The design accounted for proximity, time, vehicle capacity, construction, and volunteer safety.

Result

The response distributed 100,000 pounds of food. Routes covered about 200 locations with roughly comparable volunteer time, even when stop counts and driving distance differed.
