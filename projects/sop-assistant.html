Case study 03 · Practical AI & knowledge capture

Helping teams turn process knowledge into usable SOPs

I assisted in building SOP Assistant, a custom Microsoft Copilot agent that helps colleagues move from a conversation about how work gets done to a draft in an approved format.

ACTCross-functional collaborationImplemented and in use
~6process-design colleagues use SOP Assistant
~20SOPs and work instructions helped into draft form
~20regular attendees at biweekly practical-AI training I co-lead

The challenge

Writing down a process is harder than knowing how to do it.

Employees held useful process knowledge, but translating it into a consistent SOP or work instruction required the right questions, the right structure, and the organization’s approved format. A generic AI response would not solve that handoff.

We needed an approachable way to gather the details and produce a draft that a person could review and improve.

How it works

Structure the conversation before drafting.

  1. 01

    Gather process knowledge

    SOP Assistant asks guided questions. It can also use transcripts from non-sensitive Teams/Copilot walkthrough recordings as an input.

  2. 02

    Use approved guidance

    The custom Microsoft Copilot agent is grounded in approved SOP and work-instruction templates and associated style guidance.

  3. 03

    Produce a reviewable draft

    It organizes user-supplied information into the appropriate standard format. A human reviews the result and remains responsible for final content decisions.

My contribution

Make the tool fit the work and help people use it.

I assisted in building the agent and helped bring process-design colleagues into its practical use. Approximately six colleagues use it, and it has helped create roughly 20 new SOPs and work instructions.

Separately, I serve on a small AI-enablement team and co-lead biweekly sessions for approximately 20 regular attendees. We cover responsible use, prompting, automation, and troubleshooting across Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Power Automate, and Microsoft 365.

The document count reflects assisted drafts that remain subject to human review. Training attendance describes regular participation.

Where this translates

Practical AI adoption starts with a real workflow.

AI enablement

Find a narrow use case, give people a guided path, and explain where human judgment is required.

Knowledge operations

Capture expertise in a repeatable format so teams can review, share, and maintain it.

Change adoption

Pair a tool with training and support so it becomes usable in daily work.
