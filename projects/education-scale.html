Case study 04 · Multi-site program operations

Growing an education program across 17 schools

I co-founded and helped scale Kids on Course University from a 60-student program to one serving 750 students across 17 schools. Growth depended on repeatable operations as much as a strong mission.

The challenge

More sites create more coordination work.

Expanding an education program meant serving more students while coordinating schools, staff, schedules, families, resources, and community partners. A process that worked for 60 students could not simply be repeated informally at a much larger scale.

My role was to help build the operating practices and relationships that let the program grow across sites.

My approach

Build repeatability around the mission.

  1. 01

    Standardize the recurring work

    I developed repeatable processes for staffing, scheduling, logistics, reporting, resource allocation, and delivery support.

  2. 02

    Coordinate across organizations

    I worked with district and community partners to align the people and resources needed at multiple schools.

  3. 03

    Support the team doing the work

    I built annual tutor staffing operations for more than 20 tutors while helping coordinate program delivery across sites.

The result

Scale with a usable operating model.

Kids on Course University grew from 60 to 750 students across 17 schools. The result reflects the work of a broader team and partner network; my contribution was to co-found the program and build and coordinate the operational practices behind its expansion.

Where this translates

Growth requires more than a plan.

Multi-site operations

Create repeatable practices that can be used by people working in different locations.

Program management

Coordinate schedules, staffing, partners, resources, and delivery as scope expands.

Mission-driven transformation

Keep the purpose visible while improving the system that supports it.
