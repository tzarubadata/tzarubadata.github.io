Case study 02 · Business systems & quality controls

Connecting product changes to documentation checks

I built connected, human-controlled workflows that make enhancement information easier to track, proposed document changes easier to find, and published status easier to validate.

ACTProgram Manager IIImplemented components in use
~6 hrssaved weekly through enhancement tracking
~⅓less manual validation work for a three-person team
20+ hrssaved annually across review teams in post-publication validation

The challenge

Change information needs a path from intake to publication.

Enhancement details were fragmented across team methods. I owned a portfolio of roughly 200 documents, including some manuals longer than 150 pages. Finding relevant proposed changes by repeatedly reading dense material took substantial effort, and posted customer-facing documents needed a status check against internal records.

There was an operational problem at every stage: a reliable source of truth for enhancements, an efficient review method, and a post-publication control.

Implemented workflow

Three controls, connected by structured information.

  1. 01

    Track enhancements

    A SharePoint List supported by Power Automate and Microsoft Copilot centralizes enhancement information and ownership.

  2. 02

    Review with human judgment

    A ChatGPT-assisted workflow uses structured enhancement data and approved documentation to suggest page- and section-level changes. I validate proposals and make final decisions.

  3. 03

    Validate after publication

    A Python crawler and tracking process compare posted document status with Salesforce-derived records and flag possible discrepancies.

What changed

Less manual discovery. Better visibility into the work.

The centralized tracker saves approximately six hours per week and gives the team reusable structured input for later review. The AI-assisted workflow saves hours of manual document review, while keeping human validation and final decision authority in place.

The post-publication check reduced manual validation workload for a three-person team by about one-third and saves more than 20 hours annually across review teams. These are distinct outcomes from distinct components; I do not combine them into one savings estimate.

What’s next

Designing the next stage of the operating model.

I’m designing a review flow that would identify and mark known proposed changes before Review 1. Decision logging, cross-document impact analysis, dependency mapping, and a possible Copilot Studio migration are in development or planned.

These components remain design-stage work.

Where this translates

Business systems work is more than selecting a tool.

Business analysis

Define information ownership, requirements, and the handoffs a process needs to work across teams.

Operational excellence

Reduce fragmented tracking and manual checks while preserving a visible control path.

Responsible AI adoption

Use AI to focus human review, with approved sources and final decisions remaining with people.
